Street Etiquette Code

Color Meanings

Every collar color communicates the cat's temperament directly to neighbors and tourists alike.

Green Collar
Friendly

"Friendly. Come say hello." Loves scritches, food shares, and warm laps.

Commonly worn by sociable cats hanging around cafes, bookshops, and ferry docks.

Yellow Collar
Medium

"Medium. Approach with care." Enjoys your presence, but prefers you let them sniff first.

Great for cautious neighborhood observers who need slow hand greetings.

Red Collar
Do Not Touch

"Do not approach." Independent street boss or under medical monitoring. Admire from afar!

Protects nervous cats from unwanted touching while keeping their identity reachable via QR.
